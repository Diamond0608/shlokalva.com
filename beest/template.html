The Shipping Standards

  • Electrical schematics for the whole build, drawn in KiCad.
  • Full CAD of every part and the assembly in Fusion 360.
  • A devlog every hour of work.
  • Every hour screen-recorded, with timelapses.
  • Extensive documentation: a repo README, a bill of materials and build instructions.
